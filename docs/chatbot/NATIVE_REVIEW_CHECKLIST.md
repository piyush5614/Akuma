# Saathi native-language review checklist

This checklist is required before production claims are made for Marathi or
Hindi. Reviewers should be native speakers familiar with Maharashtra mandi
terminology.

## Review process

- [ ] Review all canned strings in `backend/app/i18n/strings.yaml`.
- [ ] Review the Marathi and Hindi knowledge bases in
      `backend/app/services/chat/kb/`.
- [ ] Run the 180-case golden evaluation and record language-specific misses.
- [ ] Test Devanagari and romanized input for crop names, units, mandi terms,
      greetings, safety, and out-of-scope requests.
- [ ] Confirm `Guidance, not a guarantee` remains clear and non-promissory.
- [ ] Confirm demo labels and data-freshness labels are understandable.
- [ ] Confirm safety and distress responses are culturally appropriate and do
      not invent helpline numbers.
